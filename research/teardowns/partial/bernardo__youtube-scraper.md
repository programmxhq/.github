## Crawler / anti-bot
- `Apify.PuppeteerCrawler`, 1 page per browser, session pool on (`YT src/main.js:103-108`).
- Blocks media, ads, analytics and YouTube telemetry URLs (`/videoplayback`, `/stats/*`, `/log_event`) (`main.js:111-131`).
- reCAPTCHA detected → `session.retire()` + retry, log advises "increasing number of proxies"; error status codes also retire session (`main.js:152-162`). No solving.
- Proxy helper mentions RESIDENTIAL/SHADER as hint groups (`YT src/utility.js:488, 520`); no group hard-coded.

## Enumeration
