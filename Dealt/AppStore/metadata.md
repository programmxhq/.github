## Description (4000)
Life deals you three cards. Play one.

Dealt is a life simulator told in chapters. Every few years you're dealt a hand of three moments: a first crush, a risky job offer, a sibling who takes your room. Play one and let the other two drift past. Some choices are safe. Some are a roll of the dice.

CHOOSE WHAT YOU LIVE FOR
Pick an ambition at birth: Fortune, Renown, Hearth, Scholar, Wanderer or Elder. It shapes the cards you're dealt and most of your score. Unlock Legacy and Thrill as you go.

FIVE STAGES, ONE LIFE
Grow from Dawn to Bloom, Build, Harvest and Dusk. Each stage brings its own cards, colours and costs. Balance Body, Mind, Heart, Bonds and money, and pick up traits that follow you for life.

WRITE YOUR EPITAPH
When the end comes, you get a rank, a score and an epitaph written from the life you actually lived. Share your tombstone with friends.

YOUR STORY OUTLIVES YOU
Pass one heirloom to your heir, like a famous name, an inheritance or a sealed letter. Your family tree grows across generations, and every life leaves something behind.

A NEW LIFE EVERY DAY
The Daily Challenge deals everyone the same life. One try. Beat your best.

• 167 hand-written cards across five life stages
• 8 ambitions, 12 achievements
• A family tree that remembers every generation
• Daily Challenge with the same seed for every player
• No ads, no accounts, no internet needed
• Made for one-handed play in short sessions
